## 2026-10-17 15:38 · Core JS
- js/audio.js: one AudioContext per take. mic → recDest, click/drone → compressor → speakers + recDest, MediaRecorder(audio/mp4 AAC → webm fallback, raw-mic fallback if mixing is unsupported), 1 s timeslice.
- Metronome: 25 ms lookahead scheduler, anchored to the user's last tap minus output latency. Re-tapping silences the old click and restores it if fewer than 4 new taps arrive.
- Tanpura: Pa, Sa', Sa', Sa pattern (1.2/1.2/1.2/2.4 s), PeriodicWave with strong upper partials + lowpass "jawari bloom" sweep, ±4-cent detuned pair, soft Sa+Pa pad. Drone register normalised to 90–280 Hz.
- js/pitch.js: YIN + median/inlier snapping. Listens until 1.5 s of voiced audio (max 5 s).
- js/db.js: stores tunes/audio/sessions/chunks. Audio kept as ArrayBuffer (safest on iOS). One retry when Safari drops the IDB connection.
- js/app.js: state machine, chunk-by-chunk persistence → crash recovery on boot, interruption handling, wake lock, persist(), share with blob prefetch on pointerdown, two-tap zip export, sequential share, zip/audio import.
- sw.js: precache + cache-first, versioned; update toast with user-triggered skipWaiting.

## 2026-10-18 12:50 · First melody analysis (tools/analyze-tune.mjs)
- User exported a 3-tune backup (Chrome, Mac) into samples/ (git-ignored). Decoded with afconvert.
- Clicks: second-difference (high-pass) energy in 5 ms frames, 40× median threshold, 120 ms min gap. The whistle (640–1600 Hz) barely registers, so clicks separate cleanly.
- Tempo: a rigid grid fit chose half-beats (172/192 BPM) and lost beats to drift. Replaced it with an adaptive beat follower seeded by the median click gap. Results: 85 / 86 / 96 BPM, ±29–56 ms wobble, steady across each take.
- Meter: click accents under 3 dB for every grouping, so it isn't readable. Suggest louder beat 1 or saying "one".
- Pitch: YIN (150–4200 Hz) on 10 ms hops, skipping ±30 ms around clicks. A global tuning offset (circular mean) shows the whistle sits −33…−39 cents from A440. Steady notes ≥ 80 ms, repeats merged, notes < 120 ms ignored for the scale.
- Sa: score = Sa time + Pa time + best scale-template coverage + last-long-note bonus − no-Pa penalty. Tune 1: A# Bilaval (good). Tune 2: C#/E/A ambiguous (relative keys). Tune 3: D# Asavari vs F# Bilaval.
- Report: samples/analysis-2026-10-18.md (git-ignored, personal).

## 2026-10-18 20:19 · v1.4.0: styles, band sounds, 16-bar A/B form
- Split Prompt into js/prompt.js (pure: GENRES ×5, FEELS ×23, GUITAR/BASS/DRUMS/ARP patterns, buildForm, buildPlan, eventsForBeat), js/instruments.js (Web Audio band) and js/prompt-player.js (scheduler + count-in).
- Form 'ab': A = each chord 2 bars (8 bars, ≤4 changes); B = a chord per bar, a colour chord from COLOURS[mode] (not already used, not the turn) at bar 7, the progression's turn at bar 8. 'loop' = old behaviour. v1.3 prompts are normalized to acoustic + loop.
- Instruments: clean KS guitar (body EQ), overdriven KS (tanh shaper + cab filters, power voicings), KS finger bass / saw+sub synth bass, drums from sine sweeps and filtered noise (kick, snare, clap, hats, open hat, shaker, crash), detuned-saw pad, square arpeggio. Separate buses: click (off by default with drums), count (always on), drums, bass, chords.
- Arrangement: damping on chord changes, muted strums for chops/palm mutes, bass approach notes, pads sustained over chord runs, a crash at each section start, a snare fill on the last beat of each section, hats doubled in B.
- Card: Style (cycles), Tempo, Sa, Chords (A compressed + B with the new chord), Feel (cycles), Form (toggles). Shuffle keeps the style; the last style is remembered in localStorage. Recording: 16-bar chord grid with the new chord outlined, section/bar label, Click/Drums/Bass/Chords toggles.
- Mix set by offline-rendering each part alone per style. Drums bus 0.45, synth bass peak 0.13, Pop chords ×1.6, Acoustic ×2, Ballad ×1.25. Result: styles within about 5 dB, peaks ≤ 0.6.
- **Test fix:** the YIN-based pluck pitch test was flaky (quiet plucks fell under the RMS gate; strong 2nd harmonics gave octave errors). Replaced it with an autocorrelation period measurement over 200 random plucks (≤5 cents).
- **Dev gotcha found:** python's http.server sends no cache headers, so the browser reused a stale prompt.js after edits and gave a false result. Added tools/serve.py (Cache-Control: no-store), pointed launch.json/npm serve at it, noted in CLAUDE.md, and re-verified everything on a fresh origin.
- Tests 28/28. v1.4.0. Committed locally, not pushed.
